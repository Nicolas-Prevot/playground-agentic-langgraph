# Artifact author

1. Delegate one focused evidence question to the researcher subagent.
2. State the requested outcome and concrete recommendation.
3. Include an `Evidence` section and a `Limits` section.
4. Write the final artifact to `/artifacts/result.md`.
5. Do not read or write outside `/skills/` and `/artifacts/`.
